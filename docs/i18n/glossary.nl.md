## Notes for the native reviewer

- Pokémon, move, place, badge and item names stay in English (PIKACHU, HARDEN, PALLET TOWN, BOULDERBADGE, OLD AMBER, POWER PLANT, HALL OF FAME, ELITE FOUR, LEAGUE), as in the English games sold in the Netherlands and Belgium.
- Trainer classes ARE translated, because they are descriptions a child should understand: JOCHIE (Youngster), MEISJE, INSECTENVANGER, KAMPEERDER, PICKNICKER, VOGELKENNER, MOTORRIJDER, GOKKER, SUPERNERD, VISSER, ZWEMMER, RIVAAL, GYMLEIDER, KAMPIOEN, LEGENDARISCH, WETENSCHAPPER. COOLTRAINER and ROCKET GRUNT stay English. Doubt: whether a Dutch child expects the English class names instead, as printed in the games.
- The computer levels follow the same choice: Jochie, Gymleider, Elite Four, Kampioen. "Jochie" for Youngster is the weakest choice; alternatives: "Jongen", "Beginner".
- "Trainerlevel" (card, chips) and "Trainerscore" (puzzle rating) mirror the English split between Trainer Level and Trainer Rating. Please check that this does not read as two different numbers.
- Pokémon pronouns: "hij" for Pokémon (JIGGLYPUFF, ARTICUNO, MEWTWO), as the Dutch anime dub does; "het" would also be acceptable.
- "Candy" is kept as in Pokémon GO in Dutch ("{family} CANDY", "+1 candy"). "Shiny" kept too.
- "Badge" kept for gym badges. The piece style option "Pokémon + badge" / "Big badge" refers to the small chess symbol on the piece, so it became "symbool" to avoid confusion with gym badges. Please confirm against the app.
- Save system: "opslag" / "opslagcode" for save / save code. Gamers might simply say "save"; reviewer's call.
- Yellow cartridge (ages 4 to 7) uses "Gelijkspel" instead of "remise", and "Tip" instead of "Hint".
- Placeholders that receive a piece or Pokémon name ({piece}, {back}, {front}) were phrased to avoid a Dutch article (de/het), since "het paard" would break "de {piece}".
- Dutch genitive on English names: BROCKS, BLAINES, LANCES, LT. SURGES (no apostrophe), MISTY'S, ERIKA'S, KOGA'S (vowel ending), ZAPDOS', MOLTRES' (s ending).
- "Team Rocket's blasting off again" became "Zo te zien vliegt Team Rocket er weer vandoor!". The Dutch dub has its own catchphrase; please replace if you know it.
- The footer disclaimer ends with "Niet aangesloten bij of goedgekeurd door hen." meaning the companies named in the previous sentence; no names were added.
